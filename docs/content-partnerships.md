## Collaboration Models `[草稿]`

三張卡片，與 RESEARCH 頁的 Research Focus 同一個版型。
以下都是合作形式的敘述，不宣稱任何既有成果。

### 1. Research Collaboration

Joint investigation on a shared scientific question, with scope, contributions
and publication expectations agreed at the outset.

### 2. Clinical Collaboration

Working with clinical groups to shape research questions around problems
encountered in practice, and to interpret findings in that context.

### 3. Technology Collaboration

Applying our laboratory and analytical workflow to questions brought by
external research groups.

> 這三種分類是我依一般生醫公司的合作型態擬的。
> **若與你們實際提供的合作方式不符，請直接改。**

## How to Start `[草稿]`

三個步驟，與 TECHNOLOGY 頁的流程版型一致。

1. **Get in touch**
   Tell us the question you are working on and what you are looking for in a
   collaborator.

2. **Scope the work**
   We discuss feasibility, what each side contributes, and what a useful
   outcome would look like.

3. **Formalise**
   Scope, responsibilities and terms are agreed before work begins.
